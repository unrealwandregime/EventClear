# Known limitations

- Independent security review and legal approval are incomplete.
- External staging, remote lifecycle, monitoring delivery, backup restore and
  rollback evidence are incomplete.
- No EventClear Polygon-mainnet contracts are deployed and no public capital is
  activated.
- Negative-risk markets, smart wallets, public registration and public LP
  deposits are unsupported.
- Relationship correctness and exceptional resolution semantics remain human
  review boundaries.
- See `docs/DEPENDENCY_EXCEPTIONS.md` for owned, expiring development-only
  advisories.
